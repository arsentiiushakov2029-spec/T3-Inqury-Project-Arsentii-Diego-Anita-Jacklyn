About Us!!

We are an independent, genre-specific bookstore located in Prospect Heights, Brooklyn. It specializes exclusively in fantasy, science fiction, romance, and young adult literature, alongside tabletop role-playing games (TTRPGs) like Dungeons & Dragons. Designed as a community "third space," the shop features a cozy backyard and hosts regular book clubs, craft nights, and gaming sessions.
